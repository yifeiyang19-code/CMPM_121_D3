# Manual test script

For a person, or an agent driving a browser. Run `deno task dev` and open the local URL with `?seed=121`. Before each section, reload. To read logical state, use `game.state` in the console. To read what has played, use the panel beside the grid, including the `#live` text, or look at the marker.

**Promotion rule:** a bug found by hand or by an agent becomes a permanent test in `core/` before it is fixed, unless it truly lives in `game/`, in which case add a step here.

| #  | Action                                                                                    | Expected observation                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | Load the page                                                                             | One canvas. A pale marker at the center of a green map. No console errors.                                                                                                                        |
| 2  | Tap the right arrow                                                                       | The marker slides one cell east. `#live` says `moved to 1, 0`. `player` is `{ x: 1, y: 0 }`.                                                                                                      |
| 3  | Walk until water or rock is ahead, then press toward it                                   | The marker lurches and flashes red, then returns to its cell. `#live` says `blocked by water at ...` for water, or `You need a torch to enter.` for rock. `player` has not changed.               |
| 4  | Tap an arrow during a walk                                                                | The tap waits. The marker finishes the walk, then takes that step. It never skips a cell.                                                                                                         |
| 5  | Mash arrows quickly, then stop                                                            | The marker settles on the cell `player` reports.                                                                                                                                                  |
| 6  | Hold an arrow down                                                                        | The marker keeps walking. Holding into a wall repeats the bump.                                                                                                                                   |
| 7  | Load `?seed=7`                                                                            | A different map.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 8  | Run `game.state` in the console                                                           | Plain data: `seed`, `player`, `inventory`, and `harvested`. No terrain list.                                                                                                                      |
| 9  | Press an arrow several times                                                              | Position and Terrain in the panel change as each step plays, and never ahead of the marker.                                                                                                       |
| 10 | Make the window short, then press the down arrow                                          | The page does not scroll. The marker moves.                                                                                                                                                       |
| 11 | Click the sound button, then press an arrow                                               | The button reads `Sound: off`, and the arrow still moves the marker.                                                                                                                              |
| 12 | Click the sound button again                                                              | The button reads `Sound: on`, and you hear a two-note chime.                                                                                                                                      |
| 13 | Tab to the sound button and press Space                                                   | It toggles. Arrow keys still move the marker.                                                                                                                                                     |
| 14 | Listen while walking and bumping (headphones or speakers)                                 | A soft tick on each step, and a low buzz on each bump. **This is the only check that a person can hear them.**                                                                                    |
| 15 | On a high-resolution display, or at browser zoom 200%                                     | Grid edges stay sharp. The marker stays centered. Run `document.querySelector("canvas").width`: it is the canvas `clientWidth` times `devicePixelRatio`.                                          |
| 16 | Load the page and read the panel                                                          | Inventory reads `empty`. Arrow keys still move the marker.                                                                                                                                        |
| 17 | Without moving, tap G (with `?seed=121` the start cell is forest)                         | The cell flashes warm light briefly. At the same moment `#live` says `harvested 1 wood at 0, 0`, Inventory reads `wood: 1`, and a short pick sound plays. The brown square stays after the flash. |
| 18 | Tap G again on the same cell                                                              | `#live` says `already harvested at 0, 0`. Inventory still reads `wood: 1`. No flash and no sound.                                                                                                 |
| 19 | Harvest the start cell, walk south to `0, 12`, then walk back                             | The start cell leaves the view and comes back still showing the brown square.                                                                                                                     |
| 20 | Tap G, then tap an arrow once while the cell is still flashing                            | The flash finishes first. Only then does the marker walk one cell.                                                                                                                                |
| 21 | Walk to another forest cell and tap G                                                     | Inventory reads `wood: 2`. Both cells show the brown square.                                                                                                                                      |
| 22 | On a grass cell, tap G                                                                    | `#live` says `nothing to harvest on grass at ...`. Inventory does not change. No sound.                                                                                                           |
| 23 | Tap an arrow, then tap G while the marker is still walking                                | The walk finishes first. The harvest happens on the cell the marker landed on, not the cell it left.                                                                                              |
| 24 | Hold G down                                                                               | Only one harvest happens until G is released and tapped again.                                                                                                                                    |
| 25 | Harvest a cell, then reload the page                                                      | Inventory reads `empty`, no cell shows the brown square, and `game.state.harvested` is `{}`.                                                                                                      |
| 26 | Without a torch, tap left 2 times and up 8 times to reach `-2, -8`, then tap up once more | The marker lurches toward the rock at `-2, -9` and returns. `#live` says `You need a torch to enter.` `player` is `{ x: -2, y: -8 }`.                                                             |
| 27 | Reload, tap right 6 times to reach `6, 0`, then tap right once more                       | The marker lurches toward the water at `7, 0` and returns. `#live` says `blocked by water at 7, 0`, not the torch message. `player` is `{ x: 6, y: 0 }`.                                          |
